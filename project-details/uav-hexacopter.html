02 — Autonomous Hexacopter

The Autonomous Hexacopter is a six-motor UAV configuration developed as part of my multi-rotor hardware and flight-control work. The platform focuses on integrating a multi-motor propulsion system with a flight controller, power-distribution network and supporting electronics to achieve stable and controlled flight.

A major part of the work involved understanding how motor placement, wiring, centre of gravity, vibration and power distribution affect flight stability.

Project details

  • I worked on the physical assembly and electrical integration of the hexacopter, including motor and ESC installation, propulsion wiring, power connections, flight-controller mounting, signal routing and pre-flight hardware checks.
  • I was involved in checking motor operation, identifying wiring and integration issues, and preparing the aircraft for practical flight testing.
  • I applied the hardware-integration practices used across the UAV development work, including clean wiring, secure connections, vibration-conscious flight-controller mounting and systematic hardware testing.
