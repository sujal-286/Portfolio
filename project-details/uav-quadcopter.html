01 — Autonomous Quadcopter | Project ASCEND

Project ASCEND is an autonomous quadcopter platform developed by Team Chanakya, designed around stable flight, autonomous navigation, telemetry, and reliable hardware integration.

The system uses an X-configuration quadcopter with BLDC motors, ESCs, LiPo power distribution, Pixhawk/ArduPilot flight control, onboard sensors, and RF telemetry.

Project details

  • I worked primarily on the hardware development and integration of the UAV, including designing and routing the electronic connections, component placement, power-distribution wiring, soldering, and complete wiring-harness assembly.
  • I also worked on flight-controller mounting and vibration isolation, using mechanical isolation to reduce vibration reaching the IMU and improve flight-state estimation.
  • I optimized signal and power wiring to reduce unwanted electromagnetic interference, particularly around the GPS and magnetometer, and worked on grounding and low-ESR filtering across the power rails.
  • I was also involved in hardware testing, troubleshooting and validating the integrated propulsion, flight-control and telemetry systems.
